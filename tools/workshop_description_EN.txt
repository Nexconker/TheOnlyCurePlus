[h1]The Only Cure+ · Field Surgery[/h1]
[b]Mod ID:[/b] TheOnlyCurePlus · [b]Bug reports:[/b] [url=https://github.com/Nexconker/TheOnlyCurePlus/issues]GitHub issues[/url]
[i]A bite is not the end. A saw, a steady hand and a bit of luck can still save you. Then you learn to live with what is left.[/i]

[b]Standalone:[/b] this is the whole mod and needs no other mods. Do [b]not[/b] enable the original The Only Cure at the same time. "Field Surgery" is part of the name, not a separate mod.

A complete fork of ZioPao's [b]The Only Cure[/b] for [b]Build 42[/b]: surgery with real risk, a body that matters, and prostheses that look like they belong on the character.

[h2]✚ New in 3.3: modular prostheses[/h2]
An arm prosthesis is now two pieces: a [b]base[/b] on the stump and an [b]attachment[/b] you can swap without taking the base off.
[list]
[*][b]5 bases[/b]: wooden, scrap, mechanical frame, medical and [b]cybernetic[/b]. Each one is modelled for a missing hand, forearm or whole arm.
[*][b]7 attachments[/b]: wooden knob, hook, mechanical hand, medical hand, cybernetic hand, chainsaw and bayonet.
[*]Some pairs do not fit: the chainsaw needs a metal base, mechanical and medical hands do not go on wood, the cybernetic hand needs a tier 3 base or better.
[*]Your old arms convert automatically into the matching base and attachment, keeping their condition.
[/list]

[h2]✚ Cybernetic tier[/h2]
[list]
[*]Tier 5: armour plates, cable muscles, segmented fingers. Cybernetic frame, cybernetic hand and a cybernetic leg.
[*]Hard to make: Welding 7, Electrical 6-7, Mechanics 5-6 and rare electronics.
[*]Best action bonus and lowest wear. The cybernetic frame gives [b]+1 Strength[/b] while worn; the cybernetic leg runs [b]10% faster[/b] than a healthy leg.
[/list]

[h2]✚ Prosthesis workbench and paint[/h2]
[list]
[*]Build the [b]prosthesis workbench[/b] (Carpentry 3) and place it like any furniture. It has a real 3D model, also for 3D/first-person camera mods such as Viewpoint.
[*]Next to it, open the workbench window: fit bases, mount attachments, see your character in 3D and compare stats with icons and bars.
[*][b]Paint your prostheses[/b] there with any vanilla paint can and a paintbrush: 10 colours for bases, attachments and legs. Scrape the paint off to get the original finish back.
[/list]

[h2]✚ Field surgery, step by step[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Examine the wound[/b] to see how far the infection has spread.
[*][b]Cure window[/b]: amputate in time and the infection is gone. Wait too long and it becomes a gamble.
[*][b]Staged procedure[/b]: disinfect, cut, saw, stitch, bandage. Each step has its tools, its time and its risk. The checklist tells you the odds before you start.
[*]Screams attract zombies. Shaky surgeons botch the job.
[*][b]Post-op[/b]: shock, convalescence, phantom pain and [b]sepsis[/b] if the stump is left dirty. Keep it bandaged and clean, or lose more.
[*]Medical professions know how; everyone else reads the [b]Field Surgery Guide[/b].
[/list]

[h2]✚ Prostheses that fit[/h2]
[list]
[*]Legs: [b]peg leg[/b] (Carpentry 3), [b]mechanical leg[/b] (Welding 4), [b]medical leg[/b] (hospital loot) and the [b]cybernetic leg[/b]. Peg legs never sprint.
[*]Wood and hooks cannot hold two-handed weapons or anything that needs fingers. With a bare base and no attachment you cannot hold anything.
[*]Mechanical, medical and cybernetic hands [b]close when gripping[/b]: weapons and tools sit in a fist.
[*]One-handed actions (pills, drinking, smoking, eating, reading...) use the hand you still have: no more ghost hand.
[*]They wear out with use. At 0 condition they fall off. Repair them. The [b]Prosthesis Familiarity[/b] skill speeds you up over time.
[/list]

[h2]✚ Weapon attachments (right arm)[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Groovy[/b]: an Ash-style chainsaw on any metal base (Welding 5, Mechanics 4). Pull the cord, refuel from any petrol can. It cuts zombies and limbs, and every zombie around hears it.
[*][b]Merle's bayonet[/b]: leather straps, duct tape and a bayonet (Welding 2). It stabs like a hunting knife.
[/list]

[h2]✚ Legs, crutches and crawling[/h2]
[list]
[*]Legs can be amputated at the foot, the calf or the thigh.
[*]One leg missing: without a [b]crutch[/b] (craftable) or a prosthesis you can only crawl. On a crutch you limp, you cannot run, and your stamina drains.
[*]Both legs missing with no prosthesis: you [b]crawl[/b], slowly, and you can still fight from the ground. Zombies will still find you down there.
[*][b]Crawler zombies[/b]: hit a zombie's leg hard enough and it drops and crawls after you (sandbox option).
[/list]

[h2]✚ Built for Build 42 and multiplayer[/h2]
[list]
[*]Body changes are validated on the server. Saves from the original The Only Cure keep working.
[*]Sandbox page [b]TOC[/b]: cure window, risk, scream radius, sepsis, convalescence, prosthesis wear and more.
[*]Modders: other mods can add their own bases and attachments with TOC_Compat.RegisterBase / RegisterHead (see API_MODULAR.md on GitHub).
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Enable [b]only one[/b] of The Only Cure or The Only Cure+: they share items and save data. Not compatible with BB First Aid Overhaul. Fancy Handwork and Brutal Handwork have no Build 42 version.
[list]
[*]Reported working: Wounds Overhaul, AHS, Viewpoint, Bandits and large modpacks in SP and MP.
[*]Safe to add to an existing save, also in multiplayer (restart the server). Back up your save before updating from 3.2: worn arms are converted on first load.
[/list]
