# The Only Cure+ (Field Surgery)

Fork of **The Only Cure** v2.4.0 by **ZioPao** (https://github.com/ZioPao/The-Only-Cure), GPL-3.0.
Original credits kept: stump/prosthesis models and textures, MP relay architecture, body-location
insertion code by RabenRabo / GanydeBielovzki, lua_timers by Vyshnia (Workshop 2875394066),
compatibility handler by Dhert.

Additions in this fork (TOC+):
- Staged field surgery (prep, anaesthesia, incision, bone, ligature, bandage) with a risk model.
- Infection window made visible: wound examination, symptoms, configurable cure window and late gamble.
- Post-operative care: sepsis through vanilla wound infection, convalescence, shock, phantom pain.
- Prosthesis tiers (wood, hook, mechanical, medical) with wear and repair.
- Leg amputations: stumps, crutch, peg leg, mechanical and medical legs, mobility rules.
- Zombies lose the limb you hit; legless zombies become crawlers.
- Severed limbs are dropped as rotting items.
- Fixes: both-side amputee traits, equip guard, cauterising at any lit fire, consumed bandages.
